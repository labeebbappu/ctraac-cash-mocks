What the voucher becomes
Two rows, one transaction:
adminExpenseVouchers
EV-0413 · maintenance · 180.0000 · paidFromCashAccountId → Doha workshop
the paper — what was bought, who signed, the receipt image
adminCashEntries
#4492 · out · 180.0000 · source adminExpenseVoucher/EV-0413
the money — the only thing the balance is summed from
A voucher with no entry is invisible to the cash book. An entry with no voucher is what the count catches. Neither is allowed to exist alone, so the entry rides the voucher's own transaction — same rule the repo already holds for audit rows.
Cancelling or deleting a voucher posts a reversal, it does not remove the entry. The reversal carries the reason and both rows keep pointing at each other.
Non-cash vouchers still work
Pick bank or card and no box is asked for and no entry is posted — the voucher records the expense and the cash book is silent, which is correct. Every voucher already in the table is exactly this case.

Variance reason required
Saving posts an adjustment entry of −25.00 carrying this reason, and only then does the book agree with the box. An adjustment is the only way the balance moves without a document — which is exactly why it must name a person and a reason.

Transfer

Moving cash without spending it — a withdrawal, a deposit, or handing a float to a technician.

Posts two entries on one transaction — out 1,000.00 from Head office, in 1,000.00 to Ahmed K. — each naming the other. Total cash in hand does not move.
A transfer is the only movement with two entries. Everything else has exactly one, and a document behind it.
A transfer never crosses currencies. A box holds one currency, snapshotted, and the totals are never blended — the same call vendor bills already made. Moving QAR into a USD box is two separate acts with a rate somebody has to state.
Bank is not a cash box
"Bank" here is a label on the entry, not a second ledger. This feature counts cash we physically hold. A full bank ledger is a bigger thing and does not need to be decided now.

Migration to the existing voucher
adminExpenseVouchers gains five nullable columns — expenseDate, currency, paymentMethod, paidFromCashAccountId, adminCashEntryId — plus adminAppId, which it does not have today. Every existing row reads as a non-cash expense, which is what they are: nothing in the table was ever paid from a box that existed.
The rules that have to hold
  • Balance is summed, never stored.
  • A posted entry is reversed, never edited or deleted.
  • The entry rides the document's own transaction.
  • One currency per box; never blended.
  • Only a finalized, non-voided receipt posts.
  • A negative balance is flagged, not refused — refusing it makes a late-entered receipt unenterable and pushes the operator off the book entirely.
